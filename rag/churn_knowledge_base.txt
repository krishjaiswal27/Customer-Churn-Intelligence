CUSTOMER CHURN ANALYSIS - KEY FINDINGS

OVERALL METRICS
Total customers analyzed: 7,032
Overall churn rate: 26.6%
Total churned customers: 1,869
Average customer monthly charge: $64.80

CHURN BY CONTRACT TYPE
Month-to-month contracts: 42.7% churn rate (3,875 customers, 1,655 churned)
One year contracts: 11.3% churn rate (1,473 customers, 166 churned)
Two year contracts: 2.8% churn rate (1,695 customers, 48 churned)
Insight: Month-to-month customers churn at more than 15 times the rate of two-year contract customers. Contract length is the single strongest predictor of retention.

CHURN BY TENURE
Customers with 0-12 months tenure: 47.7% churn rate
Customers with 12-24 months tenure: 28.7% churn rate
Customers with 24+ months tenure: 14.0% churn rate
Insight: New customers are the highest risk group. Churn risk drops significantly after the first year, suggesting the first 12 months are critical for retention efforts.

CHURN BY PAYMENT METHOD
Electronic check: 45.3% churn rate (highest risk)
Mailed check: 19.1% churn rate
Bank transfer (automatic): 16.7% churn rate
Credit card (automatic): 15.2% churn rate
Insight: Customers using electronic check have nearly 3x higher churn than those on automatic payment methods. Automatic payments correlate with lower churn, possibly due to reduced friction and commitment.

CHURN BY SPEND TIER
High spend tier (>$65/month): 34.7% churn rate
Medium spend tier ($35-65/month): 23.2% churn rate
Low spend tier (<$35/month): 10.9% churn rate
Insight: Counterintuitively, high spenders churn more than low spenders. This means the company is at risk of losing its most valuable customers, representing higher revenue loss per churn event.

MACHINE LEARNING MODEL RESULTS
Model used: XGBoost Classifier
ROC-AUC Score: 0.824
Recall for churners: 78% (correctly identifies most actual churners)
Precision for churners: 50%

SHAP FEATURE IMPORTANCE (TOP DRIVERS OF CHURN)
1. Contract type - the single biggest driver of churn predictions
2. Tenure - low tenure strongly increases churn risk
3. Monthly Charges - higher charges increase churn risk
4. Total Charges - correlated with tenure, adds additional signal
5. Online Security - customers without online security churn more
6. Tech Support - customers without tech support churn more
7. Payment Method - electronic check increases churn risk
8. Internet Service - fiber optic customers show different churn patterns than DSL

HIGH RISK CUSTOMER PROFILE
The highest risk customers share this profile: month-to-month contract, tenure under 12 months, monthly charges above $70, and electronic check payment method. Customers matching this profile show churn probabilities of 90-99%.

BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Prioritize retention offers for month-to-month customers in their first 12 months
2. Incentivize customers to switch from electronic check to automatic payment methods
3. Bundle online security and tech support services as retention tools, since their absence correlates with higher churn
4. Focus retention budget on high-spend customers since they represent the largest revenue risk
5. Consider offering discounted annual contracts to convert risky month-to-month customers
